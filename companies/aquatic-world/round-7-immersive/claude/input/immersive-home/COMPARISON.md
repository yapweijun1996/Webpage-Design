## Hard acceptance gates

1. **Continuous Home environment:** At the top, collection chapter, perspective chapter and footer, the scene still fills the visible background. No framed media, separate copy/media columns, player chrome, large Play overlay or abrupt white content band returns.
2. **Usable first impression:** Brand, offer and primary CTA are immediately available. At the standard phone sizes, the complete primary action fits the initial viewport without clipping the fish or reducing supporting text below the brief's readable size. Text enlargement and shorter windows retain ordinary scrolling.
3. **Subject and reading zones:** Full fish/fins retain safe margins throughout moving frames and viewport changes. Header, headline and controls remain readable; no copy sits over the fish's face or fin silhouette. The portrait composition has been independently reviewed, not assumed from a center crop.
4. **Real destinations:** The logo, five global links, collection action, three category entries and enquiry action reach the specified independent pages. Direct loading, refresh, Back/Forward and the actual GitHub Pages base work. Contact's category context, validation, local preview, Edit and Clear remain intact.
5. **Actual background motion:** Approved common video is loaded and advances in eligible states. Playback stays inline/silent and does not reset while scrolling. Three inspected loop joins have no visible jump, flash, doubled subject or reset. Asset defects cannot be hidden by page overlays or assigned solely to one builder.
6. **Respectful controls and fallback:** Pause/Resume, tab visibility, menu suspension, reduced motion, exposed data-saving behavior, blocked autoplay, missing media and no-JavaScript cases match the brief. Manual Pause survives temporary suspension and inner-page return. Required buttons work; blocked states remain useful poster experiences.
7. **Accessible interaction:** Keyboard navigation, visible focus, skip link, readable contrast throughout sampled frames, menu dismissal/focus return and separated touch targets work. Media is decorative; no essential text is part of the video.
8. **Independent delivery:** Builds reproduce the delivered site, resources resolve, no blocking site-origin errors appear and no forbidden sibling/input/baseline runtime import is present. Original implementations and historical input bytes are unchanged. Source, build, preview and changed files are documented.

Record each exercised gate as observed pass, observed fail or not run, with its evidence. A failed immersion/subject/reading gate blocks visual acceptance even if navigation and builds succeed. Unavailable media or device verification must stay visible in the report.

## Visual review after hard gates

Use actual-size views and moving playback, not tiny side-by-side thumbnails. Declare these weights before reviewing output; they describe this design brief only.

| Dimension | Weight | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Environmental immersion | 35% | Does the visitor feel within a continuous aquarium environment throughout Home, with no obvious player framing? |
| Composition and hierarchy | 25% | Do subject, top bar, headline and one primary action form a balanced scene with clear reading order? |
| Responsive art direction | 20% | Is the phone experience intentionally composed, and do tablet/wide-screen transitions preserve the same character? |
| Typography and detail | 10% | Are line breaks, spacing, controls, rules and navigation states deliberate and coherent? |
| Task clarity | 10% | Can a first-time visitor identify the offer and take the intended page journey immediately? |

Optional scores must include concrete viewport/state evidence and evaluator rationale. Do not average failed gates away, score unreviewed media, or claim a general model winner. The user's final judgment is separate: "Does this now feel like an immersive aquatic website rather than a video placed in a webpage?" Explicit rejection keeps design acceptance open regardless of the numerical result.
