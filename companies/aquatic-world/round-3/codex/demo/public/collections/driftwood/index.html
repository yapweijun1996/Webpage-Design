Driftwood.
Nature draws the line.

The shape of the scene.

Branching forms and weathered textures give a composition its framework. Begin with the direction of a line, and let the landscape gather around it.

Reading the material

Character is
in the irregularities.

A strong composition can begin with one distinctive form. Consider how it directs the eye, frames a view and leaves space for other elements.

01 / Line

Follow the branches

A rising branch introduces height; a low sweep suggests a wider landscape. Try different orientations in a sketch.

02 / Space

Let the gaps matter

Open areas between branches are part of the design. Think about sightlines and the relationship to the planting.

03 / Scale

Relate it to the setting

A compelling shape still needs to fit. Describe the available footprint and the intended visual balance.
