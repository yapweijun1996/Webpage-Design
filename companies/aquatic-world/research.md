## What the material establishes

- Intended design: a premium, editorial aquatic B2B homepage.
- Intended audiences: trade buyers, distributors, aquarium professionals, and visitors assessing business credibility.
- Intended journeys: collection discovery and business enquiry.
- Intended engineering behavior: configuration-driven content, responsive layout, accessible navigation, and honest enquiry state.

These are design intentions from the handoff. They do not establish that a real business has the products, facilities, operating location, certifications, or export capabilities shown in the mockup.

## What remains unverified

No independent real-company verification has been performed for this case. Company identity, domain ownership, contact routes, species labels, factual marketing claims, and production image rights remain unverified. A bounded industry information-architecture study using official sources is recorded in [CLI design lessons](redesign/LEARNINGS.md); it is not a company profile or broad industry study. The local implementation is a fictional design demo.

## Original asset limitation and shared preparation

The original supplied images are full-page design references. The handoff does not provide separate hero, category, product, or enquiry photographs. A reference image is not a substitute for an implemented webpage.

On 2026-10-10, the requester chose to prepare the same independent images before the comparison. The [shared asset pack](comparison/assets-v1/README.md) contains nine images generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, covering eleven image slots through explicit reuse. Prompts, actual dimensions, hashes, source reference, and illustrative status are recorded in its manifest. Both workspaces received identical copies under frozen input baseline v2 before either implementation started. Their initial deliveries and [evaluation](comparison/RESULTS.md) were completed on 2026-10-11, Asia/Singapore; the frozen input copies still match.

The generated scenes are interpretations of the selected reference, not real-company evidence or pixel-identical source photographs. Both builders must use these fixed sources. Differences in CSS crop, masking, image delivery, and layout can then be evaluated separately from asset acquisition.
