# Professional website design review

Apply these criteria with judgment, not as a universal style or guaranteed score. Choose density, imagery and interactions that help this audience accomplish its task.

## Product and information architecture

For each page, name the visitor question, useful content, next step and evidence. A homepage summarizes topics; an inner page adds depth and context. Keep labels understandable. Provide a coherent route from discovery to comparison or enquiry and a way back. A placeholder destination does not complete an expected journey.

## Header and navigation

Inspect at actual display size: brand legibility; baseline alignment; label size; available horizontal space; primary CTA hierarchy; separation from imagery; active, hover and focus states. Long brand names and middle-width screens should trigger a deliberate layout transition before collisions. Do not shrink navigation until unreadable to retain a desktop layout.

A small company site often needs a logo link, short set of page links and one useful CTA. Do not invent mega menus, language selectors or search without a content/task need. If the CTA and Contact share a destination, give the CTA a clear task and that page content to support it.

Mobile navigation preserves destinations and current-page context. Specify open/closed states, scrolling and keyboard behavior. A modal drawer needs focus management and dismissal; a simple disclosure does not automatically need a focus trap. Use links for destinations and buttons for actions.

## Visual craft

| Criterion | Inspect | Useful correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First viewport | Offer, focal subject and strongest action | Clarify subject and offer before adding decorative text |
| Typography | Display/body contrast, line length, wrapping, label size | Adjust hierarchy and copy together; inspect longest real labels |
| Composition | Common alignment, visual weight, consecutive bands | Establish a grid; vary emphasis by content importance |
| Imagery | Subject, aspect ratio, treatment, photo/text separation | Use deliberate crops and overlays that preserve detail |
| Rhythm | Density, white space, repetitive cards/heroes | Give story, collection index and form distinct layouts |
| Controls | Intent, hover/focus/pressed states, disabled feedback | Remove ambiguous controls; keep dimensions stable |
| Detail | Icons, rules, captions, footer and consistency | Repair a specific inconsistency across its component family |

"Impressive" is a design hypothesis: coherent brand character, purposeful composition, strong imagery and care in details may support it. User response and observed usability determine success. More animation, gradients, enormous type or decorative cards do not establish quality by themselves.

## Review format

Use concrete feedback:

> At 1024px the nav and wordmark compete for space and the CTA wraps. Switch to compact navigation before that collision, keep the brand readable, and recheck desktop, intermediate width, phone, open menu and keyboard focus.

Use pass/fail scenarios for functional acceptance. Attach full-size captures and explain tradeoffs for visual acceptance. If scoring a comparison, declare weights beforehand and separate design usefulness from reference fidelity; a fidelity score cannot establish overall website readiness.
