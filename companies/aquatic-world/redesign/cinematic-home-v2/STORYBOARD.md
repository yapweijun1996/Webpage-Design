# Standalone Film Storyboard and Review Gate

## Intended take

Produce a continuous cinematic freshwater habitat from inside the water. The camera is steady; spatial depth comes from the composition and naturally moving life. Shaded foreground leaves, middle-distance fish and wood, distant water and restrained surface light establish one coherent world. No glass, filter hardware, subtitles, logo, interface or audio.

Plan separate landscape 16:9 and portrait 9:16 takes. Suggested trial model: `runway/gen-4.5`, image-to-video, native 720p, 10 seconds, silent. Its catalog supports at most 10 seconds per request; the earlier study's proposed 12–20-second target is therefore not promised or synthesized from a shorter segment. Recheck a live quote and authorize the actual requests before submission.

| Time | Observable motion | Composition obligation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Fish begin gentle actual forward propulsion, with corresponding tail/fin action; leaves move subtly in current | Keep the environment readable; swimming space outside live copy region |
| 3–7s | Fish travel through a small natural arc at varied depths, with plausible parallax and plant occlusion | Avoid flattening the ecosystem into layered cutouts or a frozen picture |
| 7–10s | Natural movement continues without a freeze, forced pose reset or camera cut | Calm exposure and usable full-frame habitat; inspect the end/start transition honestly |

Landscape protects the shaded left reading area and keeps main fish within the right middle distance. Portrait protects the top navigation area and lower reading area; main fish remain within the central upper-middle habitat. Fish may turn, change position or pass naturally behind plants. Do not lock their reference positions, require a precise return pose or prohibit swimming.

## Runtime treatment

Review the raw forward-moving shot at normal speed before any processing. Preserve provider output and exact metadata. A web derivative may strip audio, encode for delivery, add fast-start and trim no more than 20% of the source to a usable continuous interval. Keep at least eight continuous seconds for a ten-second raw take.

Do not reverse playback, use boomerang motion, repeat held source frames, slow down a tiny segment, interpolate a fake longer take, crossfade doubled fish, or disguise failed motion with a static wallpaper effect. Poster is derived from the accepted runtime opening frame. A technically matching pair of endpoint stills is not loop acceptance.

If the forward shot visibly jumps on repetition, mark the loop **failed** and pause integration. Retain the rejected result with the reason. The next attempt or alternate treatment needs its own decision and any additional paid authorization. Do not silently spend the remaining balance on retries or publish a rejected film.

## Standalone review before either CLI

1. Watch the entire take at 1× and at full desktop/phone presentation size.
2. Observe at least three consecutive loop joins per orientation, including movement before and after each join.
3. Check body/fin continuity, propulsion, depth, plant occlusion, scene geometry, water/lighting stability and whether subject scale feels believable.
4. Check opening, middle and ending composition with the proposed live copy regions, including near-square and short landscape crops.
5. Record technical checks separately from coordinator visual judgment and the user's creative acceptance. User acceptance remains pending unless explicitly provided; this implementation authorization allows the coordinator to continue after the standalone technical and visual gates pass.

Only after the standalone technical and coordinator visual gates pass may the coordinator freeze runtime film/poster bytes for both CLI workspaces. This does not manufacture human acceptance. Source provider metadata and raw results remain local evidence; only selected reviewed runtime derivatives enter a later Pages release.
