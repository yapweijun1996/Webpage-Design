Proposed ten-second route

  1. 0–1s: fish concealed behind the root-and-leaf pocket.
  2. 1–4s: progressive emergence, then a relaxed forward swim to the shown midpoint.
  3. 4–7s: a gradual turn into depth. The full fin envelope stays clear of the navigation and reading areas.
  4. 7–9s: forward travel back behind the same pocket; occlusion covers body and fins progressively.
  5. 9–10s: fish fully concealed while subtle water and leaves remain alive.

Gold arrows show a schematic body-center path. The green box marks the intended fully visible fin corridor; partial emergence/return occurs at the pocket.
