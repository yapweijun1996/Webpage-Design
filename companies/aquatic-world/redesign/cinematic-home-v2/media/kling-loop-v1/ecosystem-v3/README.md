## Whole-scene motion intention

1. **Lead:** relaxed forward swimming, subtle tail/pectoral action and a gradual turn into depth, with persistent body volume, scales and fin structure.
2. **School:** short paths at different depths, modest individual speed/heading variation and independent movement. Do not clone poses or make all fish disappear together.
3. **Water/light:** continual gentle surface ripples and shifting refraction, consistent with the same current affecting plants.
4. **Plants:** fine tips and flexible leaves sway at varied amplitudes; heavy roots, rocks and substrate remain stable.

[preview.js](preview.js) owns schematic annotation coordinates. Native image and SVG use the same dimensions and centered cover crop. The gold, blue, pale and green annotations are direction/range suggestions. They do not detect, animate or certify movement. A future movie must independently pass appearance, anatomy, propulsion, physical occlusion, continuous habitat motion and repeated-join review. The failed fish-free identical-endpoint method is not automatically reused.
